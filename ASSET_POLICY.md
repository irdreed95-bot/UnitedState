# Corrupt State RP — 3D asset policy

The visual rebuild uses real game-ready 3D assets instead of procedural box placeholders.

- Kenney City Kit Commercial / Suburban / Car Kit: CC0.
- Quaternius Universal Base Characters: CC0.
- Assets are fetched at build time into the build workspace from public mirrors and then incorporated into the game.
- We do not redistribute the source packs as standalone files.
- Asset sources and licenses are recorded in `assets/external/ASSET_SOURCES.txt` during the build.

Visual target:
- real modular buildings
- real road/vehicle assets
- real rigged citizen model
- procedural placement/streaming around those assets
- mobile-friendly GLB assets and compatibility renderer
