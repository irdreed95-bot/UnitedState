# Unreal Asset Acquisition Policy

## Visual target

Corrupt State RP is an open-world mobile RP game. The environment must look like a real modern city, not a primitive/arcade blockout.

The uploaded Corrupt State RP design PDF remains the gameplay/world-layout source of truth.

## Sketchfab policy

Sketchfab may be used as an asset source when the individual model's current download license explicitly permits our intended use.

Preferred order:
1. CC0 / public-domain models
2. CC BY models with attribution recorded in the project
3. Other licenses only after manual license review

Never copy models from GTA, FiveM, Roblox, or other games without explicit redistribution rights.

## Performance rules

For mobile:
- Prefer modular buildings and reusable materials.
- Prefer reasonable triangle counts and texture sizes.
- Use Nanite where appropriate for supported UE targets, but do not assume Nanite solves mobile performance.
- Generate LODs and collision where needed.
- Avoid importing a complete 1M+ triangle city as the gameplay map.

## Initial environment categories

The PDF/world plan requires:
- Central government / civil services
- Bank
- Court
- Government headquarters
- Police station
- Hospital
- University
- Driving school
- Vehicle dealership
- Mechanic/garage
- Commercial downtown
- Rich residential district
- Poor residential district
- Gang district
- Coast / beach
- Forest
- Mountains
- Desert prison
- Military base
- Port

Buildings should be selected to give each district a distinct believable visual identity.
